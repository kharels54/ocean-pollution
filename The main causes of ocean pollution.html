The main causes of ocean pollution

The plastic waste

Plastic waste causes ocean pollution because it does not break down naturally, travels easily from land via rivers, and severely harms marine life through ingestion and entanglement. About 80% of this pollution comes from land-based activities due to poor waste management.

Agricultural Runoff

Agricultural runoff causes ocean pollution because rain and irrigation wash excess fertilizers, pesticides, and soil off farmland and into rivers that empty into the sea. This process introduces massive loads of nitrogen, phosphorus, and toxic chemicals that disrupt marine life

Oil Spills

Oil spills cause ocean pollution because crude oil and fuel contain toxic chemicals that do not mix easily with water, spreading rapidly and coating marine habitats, poisoning wildlife, and persisting for decades.

.Industrial Discharge

Industrial discharge causes ocean pollution because factories release toxic chemicals, heavy metals, and untreated wastewater directly into rivers and coastal waters that flow into the sea
