The impacts of ocean pollution

Creation of dead Zones

Plastic waste causes ocean pollution because it does not break down naturally, travels easily from land via rivers, and severely harms marine life through ingestion and entanglement. About 80% of this pollution comes from land-based activities due to poor waste management.

Harm to Marine Life

Ocean pollution harms marine life through physical entanglement, ingestion of toxins, and chemical-induced habitat destruction. Discarded plastics and ghost nets trap swimming animals, chemical runoff starves fish of oxygen, and toxic microplastics enter the food

Risks to Human Health

Ocean pollution directly haruman health through contaminated seafood, toxic water contact, and dangerous air-borne particles. Key hazards include heavy metal poisoning, harmful algal blooms, and bacterial infections.

Destruction of Coral Reefs

The ocean damages coral reefs primarily through ocean warming, ocean acidification, and pollution runoff. These changes cause coral bleaching, weaken skeletons, and starve marine life
